Exploring Open-Source Large Language Models — 2024

Why I Chose LM Studio

In 2024, I installed LM Studio to explore the growing ecosystem of open-source large language models that could be run entirely on my own computer. After spending time with cloud-based AI services, I became interested in understanding what was possible with locally hosted models.

One of my primary motivations was privacy. I appreciated knowing that my ideas remained on my own computer rather than being sent to external servers. As I like to joke, I didn't need big data scooping up all of my creative genius.

Discovering Open-Source Models

LM Studio made it remarkably easy to discover, download, and experiment with a wide variety of open-source language models. Whenever I read about a newly released model in AI newsletters or online discussions, I could often download it and evaluate it for myself within minutes.

I developed a growing appreciation for the rapid pace of innovation within the open-source AI community. It was exciting to have access to technology that many casual AI users never experience because they rely exclusively on commercial cloud services.

Running models locally gave me the freedom to experiment without worrying about subscription limits, usage caps, or internet connectivity after the models had been downloaded.

Advantages of Local AI

One of the biggest benefits of using LM Studio was that everything stayed on my own computer. Beyond the privacy advantages, local execution also meant I could continue experimenting even when offline. The experience felt more like owning development tools than consuming an online service.

Because there were no message limits or quotas imposed by a hosted provider, I was free to test prompts, compare models, and spend as much time learning as I wanted. This encouraged experimentation.

What I Learned

Installing LM Studio broadened my understanding of the AI ecosystem beyond commercial platforms. I gained firsthand experience with the diversity of open-source models and learned that many capable language models are freely available to anyone with suitable hardware.

The experience also reinforced the importance of open-source software within the AI community. Being able to explore new models shortly after their release gave me a deeper appreciation for how quickly the field evolves and how collaborative many AI projects have become.

Reflection

My experience with LM Studio marked another step in my AI journey. Running models locally gave me greater control, strengthened my understanding of modern language models, and allowed me to experiment with cutting-edge open-source AI on my own terms.

I continue to see local AI as an important part of the broader AI landscape. While cloud-hosted services offer convenience and access to extremely large models, locally hosted models provide privacy, flexibility, and the freedom to experiment without restrictions.
