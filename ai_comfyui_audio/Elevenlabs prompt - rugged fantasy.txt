Elevenlabs prompt - rugged fantasy dwarf

"Generate the voice of a rugged fantasy dwarf: a deep, gravelly baritone with a rough, weathered texture that suggests decades spent in mountain halls, forges, and battlefields. The voice is broad, powerful, and resonant, with a naturally gruff delivery that carries warmth beneath its stern exterior. Speak with measured confidence and deliberate pacing, emphasizing consonants slightly and using a rich chest resonance. The tone should convey resilience, pride, craftsmanship, and quiet determination rather than anger. Add subtle breaths and natural vocal imperfections, such as occasional gravel and rasp, while maintaining excellent intelligibility. The accent should evoke a classic high-fantasy mountain culture without closely resembling any specific existing fictional character or copyrighted performance. Deliver the speech with the presence of a seasoned clan elder or master smith telling a story beside a roaring forge."
