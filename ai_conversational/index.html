Exploring Conversational AI Through Interactive Storytelling

Evaluating Early Large Language Models — 2023

Curiosity Beyond Image Generation

After experimenting with AI-generated artwork in 2022, I became interested in the rapidly growing field of conversational AI. Throughout 2023, large language models were receiving enormous public attention, with discussions ranging from their creative potential to speculation that they might one day surpass human intelligence.

Rather than relying on headlines, I wanted to evaluate these systems for myself. I was particularly interested in whether they could serve as engaging interactive storytellers or even replace a traditional tabletop game master.

I subscribed to Perplexity Pro which allowed me to swap out various models all from the same same interface.

AI as a Dungeon Master

One of my first experiments was seeing whether a chat model could act as the Dungeon Master for a Dungeons & Dragons campaign. I tried a variety of community-created prompts that were specifically designed to guide the model into managing a fantasy role-playing adventure.

Initially, the results were promising. The AI was capable of describing locations, introducing characters, and responding creatively to player actions. For short interactions, it created an experience that felt surprisingly immersive.

However, as the adventure continued, limitations became increasingly apparent. The model struggled to remember important details from earlier in the story, often forgetting inventory items, character abilities, or previous events. It could improvise well in the moment, but maintaining consistency over an extended campaign proved difficult.

Interactive Storytelling

I also experimented with prompts designed to create "choose your own adventure"-style experiences. My goal was to explore immersive fantasy stories set in classic swords-and-sorcery worlds where my choices would influence the direction of the narrative.

The AI could certainly generate imaginative scenes and interesting plot ideas, but maintaining a coherent world over time was challenging. Characters occasionally behaved inconsistently, details changed unexpectedly, and the story would sometimes lose track of earlier events. These issues made it difficult to remain fully immersed in the adventure.

Although the models demonstrated impressive creativity, they often struggled with the long-term memory and consistency needed to support complex interactive games.

Comparing Reality to the Hype

One of the most interesting aspects of these experiments was comparing my hands-on experience with the public conversation surrounding AI at the time. There was widespread speculation that AI systems were on the verge of becoming autonomous or even posing an existential threat.

My own experience painted a much more grounded picture. Rather than behaving as an unstoppable intelligence, the models were surprisingly easy to distract. They frequently forgot important context, lost track of ongoing objectives, and needed regular guidance to stay focused on the task at hand.

The contrast between the media narrative and my personal experience reinforced the importance of evaluating technology firsthand rather than relying solely on popular opinion.

Lessons Learned

Although I ultimately came away somewhat disappointed by the limitations of the technology at that point in time, the experience deepened my appreciation for both the strengths and weaknesses of large language models. I learned that they excelled at generating creative ideas and engaging dialogue but struggled with maintaining persistent state across long, interactive sessions.

Those observations also helped shape my later AI projects. As newer models became available, I found myself returning to many of the same questions, curious to see how improvements in reasoning, memory, and context handling were addressing the limitations I had encountered in 2023.

Reflection

Looking back, these experiments served as an important transition between my initial curiosity about generative AI and the more technical projects that followed. Instead of viewing AI as a novelty, I began evaluating it as a technology with measurable strengths, identifiable limitations, and tremendous potential for future improvement.

The experience taught me an important lesson that continues to guide my approach to AI today: meaningful understanding comes from hands-on experimentation. By testing new tools for myself, I can develop informed opinions based on practical experience rather than expectations or hype.
